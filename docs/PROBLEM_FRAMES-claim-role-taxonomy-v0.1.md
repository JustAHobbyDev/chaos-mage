### Requirements (per frame, with explicit out-of-scope)

### Invariance test

Git blobs, hashes, committed input allowlists and isolated packets stabilize local
inputs. Provider availability, serving snapshots and account usage can change;
pause on events, record exposed facts and uncertainty, never invent stability.
One judgment per item cannot establish repeatability. This bounded study measures
observed assignability and routing, not universal validity of the taxonomy.

### Stakeholder test

The research operator needs independently preserved measurements and explicit
ambiguity. H.6's generic warrant question and permanent provider-failure rule do
not transfer to this newly authorized taxonomy study. No product analogy is used.

### Open questions — decided here, with reasoning

balance. Target 21, maximum 24 natural cases; report missing roles without synthetic
replacements. A mixed-function frozen claim is not split. Source/limit and
conditional/inference overlap receive no invented priority. Serial calls and
three-session checkpoints bound exposure. Provider events pause without retries
or missing-observation verdicts. Cost/usage unknowns require concrete approval.
