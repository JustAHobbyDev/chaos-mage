# Problem Frames — Experiment E

## Entry #1 — 2026-09-29 — Natural generation and independent neutralization
### Domains

Read-only lexical inputs: historical experiments, accepted instruments and six fixed
baselines. Experiment-owned artifacts: frozen assignments, generated mappings, blind
paraphrases, audits, packets, raw calls, immutable results and review. External model
services vary independently and are accessed through isolated causal CLI interfaces.
The operator freezes procedures and interprets results only after measurement.

### Frame split

Preparation transforms fixed inputs into isolated stage packets. Commanded collection
obtains natural mappings, paraphrases, fidelity audits and judgments. Post-freeze
transformation and research review compute descriptive evidence and inspect mistakes.

### Requirements (per frame, with explicit out-of-scope)

Mappings must be generated without knowledge of the gate or other cases. Paraphrases
must preserve operational meaning without seeing baseline or verdicts. Both audits
must pass before primary admission, and both unchanged validity judgments must be
Valid. Reject only clear collapse; preserve uncertainty. No ranking, grounding,
diversity, retrieval or production integration; no tuning or replacement outputs.

### Invariance test

Hashes and commit ancestry fix inputs and chronology. Practitioner consensus and
hidden serving snapshots remain unverified. Fidelity is judged, not established by
agreement. Record metadata and preserve failures instead of implying determinism.

### Stakeholder test

The researcher needs evidence about preserving strange valid reasoning, with false
rejection costly. Stateful assistance or repaired outputs would alter the object under
test. Fresh contexts, bounded interpretation and inspection of every rejection serve
that stakeholder. No borrowed product analogy determines the design.

### Open questions — decided here, with reasoning

- User selected all fourteen accepted instruments, two or three assignments each.
- User selected continuation after generation/neutralization content exclusions;
  provider/isolation and judge-format failures retain the historical scheduling halt.
- Use two audit families; require all preservation checks from both. No adjudication.
- Preserve unspecified rules as unspecified, without inventing missing steps.
- No synthetic evidence bridges, replacement generations or duplicate controls.
- Missing minimum coverage yields an insufficient-coverage publication and stops.
- User authorizes implementation through normal push; no further approval gate.

### Carried forward

Small purposive sample, baseline scope, provider uncertainty, audit conservatism and
generator/neutralizer coupling limit interpretation. Recommend only a next step.
