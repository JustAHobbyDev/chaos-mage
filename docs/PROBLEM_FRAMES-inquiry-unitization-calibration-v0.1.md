Frozen text and grammar are stable. Model behavior is independently variable: measure once
and do not repair. A bounded grammar cannot guarantee coverage of unrestricted language.

Researcher needs coverage evidence and negative results, not manufactured agreement.
H.3's negative-candidate eligibility is unsuitable for semantic inquiry discovery.

strict one-role coverage and deduplication by full decision structure. Rejected parseable
judgments stay recorded; provider/schema failures stop without retry. Remove all textual
formulations for the whole-unit counterfactual. User authorizes creation, calls and push.
