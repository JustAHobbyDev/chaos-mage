# H6.R4 frozen routing and contracts

For a material primary or embedded component:

| Origin | Function | Assertion mode | Route |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| TARGET_SUPPLIED | OPERATION | ASSERTED_COMPONENT | TARGET_FIDELITY + OPERATION_LICENSE |
| SOURCE_SUPPLIED | OPERATION | ASSERTED_COMPONENT | SOURCE_FIDELITY + OPERATION_LICENSE |
| TARGET_SUPPLIED or SOURCE_SUPPLIED | Existing function | REFERENCED_PREMISE | GROUNDING_REF; no fresh scientific obligation |

REFERENCED_PREMISE means unchanged context/premise at the same scope and strength.
ASSERTED_COMPONENT means the mapping actively prescribes an operation, selects it
as the next action, applies it to the target, endorses it as part of the transfer,
or materially transforms/extends the supplied proposition. Supplied origin never
exempts a newly asserted use from functional scrutiny. Positive supply evidence
must establish the actual component, not merely a related source idea. Do not
mislabel generated target-specific additions as supplied.

This is the sole scientific routing correction. No new origin/function categories,
governance redesign, remainder redesign or dependency expansion. A candidate
requiring them is excluded before measurement.

## TARGET_FIDELITY

Is this proposition explicitly supplied by the frozen target/problem material, or a faithful non-expansive restatement of it at the same scope and certainty?

## SOURCE_FIDELITY

Is this proposition explicitly contained in, or a faithful non-expansive restatement of, the frozen source instrument and its stated mechanism, conditions, or limits?

## OPERATION_LICENSE

Does this proposed observation, intervention, comparison, or transformation faithfully instantiate the transferred mechanism at the target, under the stated applicability conditions, and does it produce information or action relevant to the target problem?

These contracts are unchanged from H6.R3. Verdicts are SATISFIED, CONDITIONAL,
VIOLATED and UNCERTAIN. Aggregate measured obligations by
VIOLATED > UNCERTAIN > CONDITIONAL > SATISFIED. Missing results stay null;
GROUNDING_REF is a resolution, not an automatic SATISFIED judgment.
