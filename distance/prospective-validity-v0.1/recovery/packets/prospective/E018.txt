# Prospective transfer validity v0.1

Judge only the supplied source instrument, target question, and unchanged mapping.
The mapping proposes a procedure; it need not have been executed. Do not use tools,
external knowledge retrieval, repository context, historical judgments, or imagined
data. Do not repair the mapping, add an evidential bridge, invent a discriminating
measurement, or strengthen its stated claim. Return one JSON object conforming to
the supplied schema, with the exact case_id. All required fields must be present.

Assess three validity links separately from execution readiness.

## Mechanism fidelity

Question: Assuming required execution resources can be obtained, does the mapped
state → operation → signal chain preserve the source instrument's distinctive
operational mechanism?

- `preserved`: the mapped chain coherently instantiates that mechanism.
- `conditional`: a specific unresolved mechanism condition in the mapping determines
  whether the chain instantiates it. Name that condition and its functional role.
- `not_preserved`: the mapped chain fails to preserve the mechanism; acquiring
  resources or merely running the proposed steps does not fix it.

Engineering ability to build a configuration, obtain access, or operate an instrument
is ordinarily execution readiness. Whether an intervention actually changes the
intended variable, a sequence has the required structure, or an operation implements
the source's defining transformation can be a mechanism issue. Identify the role;
the words “check,” “verify,” and “establish” do not decide the category. Do not require
a favorable observed result or guarantee that every application will resolve the
target when the procedure explicitly permits unresolved or bounded outcomes.

## Warrant validity

Question: If the mapped operation produces the stated signal under the mapping's
explicit conditions, does that signal support the stated inference?

**Assume all execution prerequisites are satisfied and the stated signal occurs.**
Does the signal then warrant the inference? If not, use `unsupported` unless the
mapping itself supplies a specific unresolved empirical relation that could
establish the warrant. In that latter case use `conditional`, identify that relation,
and explain why it would support this inference if established. Merely naming a gap
or saying that evidence should be checked does not supply the missing relation.

- `supported`: the stated signal justifies the stated bounded inference through the
  mapping's specified evidential relationship. No substantive warrant gap remains.
- `conditional`: a specific unresolved empirical warrant relation supplied by the
  mapping could establish this signal-to-inference bridge. Explain the actual
  dependency, without inventing a new method or adding a missing control.
- `unsupported`: even with execution prerequisites satisfied and the stated signal
  obtained, the mapping supplies no adequate bridge to the inference, nor a specific
  unresolved empirical relation that could establish it.

Preserve the distinction between observing a pattern and interpreting it. Causal
contribution, chronological direction, localization, specificity, necessity and
interaction claims each need their own evidential bridge at the claimed resolution.
Do not treat circular support, non-identifiability, confounding, association promoted
to causation, or a detected feature promoted to historical proof as mere execution
preconditions. An outcome defined using the desired conclusion does not independently
support that conclusion. Stability or repeated agreement alone does not rule out a
shared error or an alternative explanation.

Ordinary instrument setup, event logging, parameter specification and data acquisition
can be readiness. Independence, construct meaning, comparability, feature specificity
and resolving power can instead determine what the signal means. Calling such inputs
“qualified,” “reliable,” or “available” does not establish a warrant. Selecting a
window or threshold is different from establishing that it supports a claimed bound.
Explain the functional distinction for this mapping rather than applying a word rule.

A coherent prospective test need not already have yielded a positive result. Do not
make missing observations alone a warrant defect or convert every unperformed step
into a substantive condition. A planned, properly specified discriminating test can
support a bounded conditional inference from its possible signal. Conversely, a
promised future check cannot launder a missing bridge into readiness: assess whether
the mapping actually specifies the relationship that could justify the inference.

## Target fidelity

Question: Does the mapped inference provide a justified answer or bounded contribution
to the retained target question?

- `addressed`: the inference has an explicit, justified relationship to the target.
- `conditional`: a specific unresolved target-fidelity bridge supplied by the mapping
  determines whether the bounded inference addresses the retained target.
- `not_addressed`: the inference fails to address the retained target, even as a
  justified bounded contribution.

Do not require complete resolution of a broad question. Preserve stated scope:
relative versus absolute chronology, test versus production behaviour, prototype
versus actual behaviour, a candidate for investigation versus established causation,
and tested configurations versus all possible configurations are distinct claims.
Do not invent a stronger claim and reject the mapping for failing to establish it;
do not silently weaken a stronger stated claim to rescue the mapping either.

## Conditions and execution readiness

List material unresolved conditions on which your judgment relies. For each, provide
the condition, category, rationale, and effect_on_validity (`none`, `conditional`, or
`invalidating`). Categories:

- `execution_precondition`: ability to run an otherwise coherent procedure; satisfying
  it does not change the mechanism, warrant, inference or target. Always effect `none`.
- `mechanism_condition`: truth determines whether state → operation → signal preserves
  the source mechanism.
- `warrant_condition`: truth determines whether signal → inference is justified.
- `target_fidelity_condition`: truth determines whether inference → target is justified.
- `mixed`: inseparable substantive roles remain; explain which links are involved.
- `uncertain`: supplied context cannot settle the condition's role; explain what is
  missing. Do not use uncertainty to hide a known unsupported inference.

Separate conditions with different roles where possible. A compound requirement does
not become execution-only simply because one part is resource availability. A
condition's method of verification does not determine its category. Do not invent
additional requirements or redesign the source mapping.

Execution readiness is descriptive and never independently changes final validity:

- `ready`: no unresolved execution prerequisites are identified; preconditions is empty.
- `requires_preconditions`: list each execution prerequisite with how_to_establish and
  why_execution_only. The list must exactly match the execution_precondition entries
  in conditions. These conditions have effect_on_validity `none`.
- `unknown`: explain what cannot be determined; do not use this status when known
  execution prerequisites have been identified. Preconditions is empty.

For example, lack of access to a box can prevent a specified visible-token count from
running without defeating the counting inference. Obtaining access cannot make that
count establish who made the tokens. A separately specified but unestablished
calibration relation between a measured height and volume can be a substantive
conditional warrant; do not invent such a relation when the mapping supplies none.

## Final status and consistency

- `Invalid` if mechanism_fidelity is `not_preserved`, warrant_validity is `unsupported`,
  or target_fidelity is `not_addressed`. Failure takes precedence over conditionality.
- Otherwise `Conditional` if any of those three components is `conditional`.
- Otherwise `Valid`: mechanism `preserved`, warrant `supported`, target `addressed`.

Execution readiness may be any of its three statuses for a Valid mapping. Execution
preconditions alone never make a mapping Conditional or Invalid. A conditional
component requires a corresponding substantive conditional condition (or explained
mixed condition); an invalidating condition requires an Invalid final status. Do not
duplicate conditions or contradict component statuses in the condition list. State
remaining uncertainty plainly. Evaluate these validity links only; do not apply
other admission, ranking or aesthetic criteria.
