push and selected two input freezes, 28 planned primary pairs, six reversal controls,

## Question and domains

Does direct comparison provide coherent resolution for preferentially exploring
mappings that change ordinary target inquiry, without selecting exotic vocabulary?
The experiment measures isolated provider judgments of authored hypotheses, not
practitioner consensus or a production retrieval policy. Historical records and
accepted instruments are immutable lexical inputs. New candidates and baselines are
researcher workpieces; guarded scheduling is commanded measurement; admission and
metrics are deterministic transformations; interpretation remains researcher review.
Provider behavior and actual ordinary practice move independently of these records.
No immutable serving snapshot is claimed without exposed evidence. No borrowed
product analogy determines the design; the stakeholder needs inspectable disagreement.

## Preregistered analysis

Normalize both overall and criterion A/B relations and mapping-specific diagnostics
to canonical identities before comparing families. Report the four-way confusion
matrix and exact agreement; directional same/opposite counts; direction-versus-tie,
tie-versus-tie and all indeterminate combinations; each criterion separately; each
mapping-occurrence counterfactual and local/global agreement; decisive-criterion exact
agreement plus qualitative compatibility. Repeated mapping occurrences are dependent:
report their identities and within-family status distributions, never average them
into a replacement judgment or treat occurrences as independent mapping samples.

For each complete measured triangle and family, any indeterminate edge makes the
triangle indeterminate. Without ties, a directed 3-cycle is cycle and an acyclic
orientation is transitive. With ties, classify tie-compatible and separately report
tie-substitution tensions (e.g. two ties with a strict third edge, or inconsistent
directions to tied endpoints). A strict chain whose closure is tied is tie-compatible,
not repaired. Also list longer simple directed cycles in the measured graph. Never
infer unmeasured edges or require a total order. Controls are excluded.

For every control compare normalized overall relation, all criteria, counterfactual
and scope diagnostics with its primary original, separately by family. Changes may
reflect presentation sensitivity or single-draw variability; they do not identify
a causal order effect in isolation.

Post-freeze review categories: indeterminate if either verdict is indeterminate;
contested-direction for opposing directions; order-vs-tie for one direction and one
tie; stable-tie for two ties; close-order for matching directions if either is close
or decisive rationales are incompatible; otherwise stable-order. Different primary
criteria default to close-order and cannot be promoted by a compatibility judgment.
Compatibility reviews may downgrade same-criterion clear cases to close-order.
Original outputs remain untouched. A substantive missing-information explanation is
required for interpreting indeterminate; unsupported uses are explicitly flagged.

Only after the comparison freeze, join historical B labels for reused/reused pairs.
Describe same-class resolution and different-class ties for each historical family;
do not invent historical labels for new mappings or calculate accuracy against B.
Review every pair, particularly opposing directions, source/vocabulary shortcuts,
evidence access, unsupported baseline assumptions, local/global confusion, warrant
versus inference strength, criteria/verdict tensions and repeated counterfactual
contradictions. No agreement threshold or classifier tuning follows these findings.
