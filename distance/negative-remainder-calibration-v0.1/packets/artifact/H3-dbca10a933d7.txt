# H.3 frozen productive-negative-remainder rule

A negative remainder is productive only when it changes the post-ablation target
inquiry by excluding or bounding target states, changing the justified priority of
live alternatives, establishing a stopping condition, or selecting a specific
discriminating next inquiry. A statement that merely reports failure to establish a
conclusion, without changing those states or inquiry decisions, is evidentiary
insufficiency and does not by itself constitute a viable remainder.

**Cannot conclude X is not equivalent to X is excluded.** Absence of support is not
evidence against a hypothesis. Negative evidence can be useful without asserting a
positive conclusion. Source terminology and warning importance do not establish
productivity.

## Counterfactual and roles

For each negative candidate R, remove R while leaving every other surviving element
unchanged. What materially changes? Inspect target state set, bounds, alternative
priorities, stopping decision, and selected next inquiry. Do not assume that removing
a redundant conclusion also removes separately surviving evidence for it. Source
citations justify a candidate; they are not permission to delete other evidence.

A target state becomes live again, a bound widens/disappears, or relative alternative
priorities change: productive YES, primary role TARGET_CONSTRAINT. A stopping decision
or specific discriminating next inquiry changes: productive YES, primary role
INQUIRY_CONSTRAINT. Record the strongest/primary role and all secondary effects.
If all five remain unchanged: productive NO, role INSUFFICIENCY_ONLY. If supplied
content cannot establish the decisive effect: productive UNCERTAIN, role UNCERTAIN.

An inquiry requires all four: an unresolved contrast; a concrete next operation,
observation, record or test; an explanation of how possible outcomes discriminate
among the live alternatives; surviving mapping provenance for every component.
All must already be supplied. Missing components mean productive NO, unless their
availability genuinely remains uncertain. Generic advice to collect evidence,
investigate further or verify a result does not qualify. A stopping decision must
terminate a branch for a cited source-specific reason. The next-operation components
may be inapplicable to a stopping-only candidate; explain the stopping condition.

## Viability and artifact derivation

Assess every candidate in this order, using YES / NO / UNCERTAIN with rationales:
1. warranted: licensed by surviving mapping content;
2. source_derived: an operational consequence of the imported instrument;
3. target_relevant: bears on the exact retained target or a legitimate bounded part;
4. productive: transforms the justified target inquiry;
5. material: that productive contribution has useful consequence, however small.

Do not reason important warning -> material -> viable. A candidate is viable exactly
when all five are YES. A candidate is decisively unresolved when no field is NO and
at least one is UNCERTAIN. Uncertainty cannot rescue an already disqualified candidate.
Productivity applies to nonnegative candidates too; negative-role labels apply only
to negative inferences. Inventory membership does not prejudge any assessment.

At least one definite viable remainder yields KEEP_WITH_WARRANT_FLAGS for
substantially_intact scope, or KEEP_WITH_REDUCED_SCOPE for reduced scope. Without any
definite viable remainder, decisive unresolved viability yields UNCERTAIN_LOAD_BEARING;
otherwise CORE_INVALID. INSUFFICIENCY_ONLY cannot save an artifact by itself.
Generator-facing uncertainty remains keep + flag.

Mechanism survival, central bridge, dependency cascade, text volume and ownership are
diagnostics, never substitutes for viability. Explain structural conflicts without
overriding the five-property derivation. Reasons are none for keep, uncertain for
unresolved, otherwise mechanism_death, generic_remainder, target_payoff_loss or
epistemically_empty. CORE_INVALID has scope none.

Assess every frozen candidate unchanged. Additional candidates require origin
judge_added and exact surviving mapping citations. Direct entailment is allowed;
invented repair, replacement inference and future evidence are not. Assess every
negative candidate in negative_remainder_assessment and cross-reference its ID.
The strongest inference copies one assessed candidate or is null. A strongest
candidate is not automatically viable. No hidden authoring hypothesis is an answer key.

Assess the ablated mapping, not the removed claims. Copy every frozen candidate and citation exactly. Assess all five viability properties in the prescribed order. Mark whether each candidate is a negative inference and provide negative_remainder_assessment for every negative candidate. All frozen candidates here are plausible negative inferences; if you judge otherwise, explain that in its productivity rationale. Additional candidates must be marked judge_added and cite surviving mapping excerpts. For inquiries, cite surviving mapping spans for the contrast, operation and outcome relation. Citation source_field names refer to original mapping fields, but deleted spans are unavailable as evidence. Do not infer an intended answer from inventory membership. Empty deletion sets are valid. Derive status from the five-property rule; structural diagnostics cannot override a definite viable remainder.
