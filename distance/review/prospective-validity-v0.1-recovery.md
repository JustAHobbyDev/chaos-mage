### Warrant-laundering and mandatory diagnostics

**E006 — anchor diagnostic: failed.** The mapping states that if H1 (steering) and
H3 (unintentional irregularity) both survive, the defensible statement is that the
process was irregular. They can both survive alongside H2 (regular merit-based
process) when the evidence is non-discriminating. Even after all execution
prerequisites are satisfied and the comparative matrix is observed, their survival
does not establish irregularity. Access, authentication, a second rating and
sensitivity analysis do not close that logical gap. The new response describes a
bounded procedure but omits this stronger claim, assigning supported warrant and
Valid. The old Invalid rationale identifies the specific counterexample. This is a
material warrant false acceptance by overlooking or softening a claim, even though
the new execution-only conditions themselves are reasonably limited to resources.
The measured Valid remains unchanged and remains in the measured eligible count.

**E030 — anchor/circularity diagnostic: passed rejection.** It remains Invalid.
A perfectly continuous, truthful post-compositional ledger may document sorting or
rebinding, so accounted-for handling does not imply the current arrangement is the
author's final arrangement. The new judge also identifies the circularity of ranking
histories by fewest breaks when reconstructed ledger lines use the same interpretive
traces being assessed. Baseline description is kept in readiness; neither missing
resources nor missing observed outcomes explain this invalidity.

**E022 — hard stress diagnostic: passed rejection.** It remains Invalid.
Response dead time and settling describe dynamics, not necessarily local accumulated
waiting; downstream delay may be propagated from an upstream queue. Transient
overshoot can coexist with eventual improvement, and a prompt response after dead
time does not exclude capacity shortage. Reversal and repetition cannot supply the
missing diagnostic specificity. This success cannot independently determine F's
success, especially given E006's failure.

A separate condition-label concern occurs in **E015**: the new execution prerequisite
bundles obtaining records with their authentication. Authentication is relevant to
the evidential warrant, not merely availability. E015 remains Invalid for the
unsupported ranking of checkpoints by award contribution, so that concern does not
change admission. The audit does not infer that all practical prerequisites are
scientifically innocuous just because their labels say execution.

Further boundary inconsistencies remain. E005 treats establishing a reproducible
starting failure as a built-in start/stop test; E028 makes its analogous baseline/full-
delta reproduction a mechanism condition. E013/E027 credit bounded corroboration
procedures, whereas E016 retains reliability as an unresolved warrant condition.
E004/E021/E025 apply different thresholds to guarded pilot support. Functional
analogy versus source mechanism also remains sensitive to interpretation, including
E007 and the three chromatography mappings. These are operator qualifications,
not new measurements, repaired labels or reasons to rerun F.

## Conclusion, limits and next step

Experiment F's authorized recovery and measurement collection are complete. The
scientific result is **mixed and insufficient to validate this classifier as a
reliable admission rule**. Execution readiness can be separated usefully from
prospective validity, and several previously conditional procedures receive
defensible Valid readings. However, E006 demonstrates that strict wording alone did
not prevent a material warrant false acceptance; taxonomy and prospective boundary
inconsistencies remain. E030 and E022 show that the classifier can reject some real
warrant defects, not that it reliably rejects all of them. The eligible count is below
the proposed floor. Neither count growth nor one successful stress diagnostic proves
success.

Limitations include the fixed 30-case corpus, one Astra observation per case,
non-independent overlapping taxonomy conditions, authored domain/mapping assumptions,
no observed target-domain outcomes, and operator review with prior context. There is
no within-model stability measurement, no cross-family robustness measurement, and no
independent human ground truth. Changes from v0.3 combine prompt, schema and framing
changes with sampling variation. A requested model alias and pinned executable do
not prove an immutable served backend snapshot: the captured responses expose no
returned model identifier or verified served snapshot. Isolation/non-contamination
proof rests on recorded local execution and raw events, not unobservable provider
internals.

**Recommended next step, not executed:** obtain separate authorization for an
independent claim-level audit and a separately versioned evaluation of the identified
boundary rules. Preserve these results. Do not tune or rerun F, reopen E, or start
anti-collapse on the strength of this run.
