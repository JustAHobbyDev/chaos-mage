# Experiment B.2 — common-evidence comparative displacement

Completed 2026-09-28. **The partial-order vocabulary captures a substantive tradeoff, but common evidence does not yet produce a stable comparative research control.** Identical starting packets remove the literal unequal-input confound. They do not eliminate divergent interpretations of evidence, native practice or task reach. Keep this representation for inspected research; it is not ready to steer creative search autonomously.

The strongest negative findings are persistent family-specific meaningfulness thresholds, task-reach agreement of only 3/10 prospectively and 2/10 consequentially, a consequence presentation-reversal change, and material outcome-authoring concerns. One rollout world's initial input state is underspecified despite identical packet bytes. Positive probe evidence versus largely redundant matrix evidence, and unequal completeness of written branch analysis, also limit interpretation. These concerns are retained without repairing, replacing or deleting measured cases.

This report interprets frozen outputs; it does not adjudicate correctness by agreement. Operator intentions are not reference answers. No success percentage was defined. Experiment C and retrieval integration were not executed.

## Design, common worlds and admission

The preregistered design used eight unchanged B.1 baseline files, 20 new mappings and 16 planned pairs. Four targets had complete three-mapping triangles and four had single pairs. This supports limited cycle tests without inflating every target into a large comparison graph. After validity attrition, **10 primary pairs across six targets** survived, plus four orientation controls. Sixteen mappings passed Valid/Valid; 14 participate in surviving pairs and two are orphaned.

Worlds were constructed and committed before mappings: target question and native baseline, one hidden world, then its observable initial evidence, affordances, constraints and budget. Mappings were authored against that frozen environment. Their operations select outcomes through `derive.py`, which records accessed world facts, resource use, resulting signals, analysis and limits. Regeneration and hashes detect changes to worlds, operations and derivation logic. The checker verifies provenance and explicit transforms, not scientific truth. In particular, finite response arrays and qualitative record classifications are author stipulations.

Every target gives both mappings one primary procedure, at most one immediate followup and zero external sources. A procedure may contain elementary trials, charged to the same target-specific ledger. Unused resources are allowed. Exogenous evidence is the common observable packet; endogenous evidence includes new experiments and new patterns extracted from shared records. Judges never receive hidden operator truth.

[Component hash proof](../common-evidence-displacement-v0.3/review/diagnostics.json) covers all 14 pair packets, including controls: target, native baseline, facts, artifacts, initial observations, known uncertainties, affordances, constraints and budget are identical between branches. Common-state hashes and world IDs are also checked against every outcome. Both families receive identical packet bytes for a given case. All 20 outcome records regenerate, including excluded mappings. This establishes serialized identity and absence of undeclared mutation. It does **not** establish physical equivalence where the authored starting-state semantics are ambiguous, as in T08.

The unchanged v0.3 validity gate ran independently in both families. Family A returned 20 Valid. Family B returned 16 Valid and four Conditional; neither returned Invalid. No Conditional mapping entered displacement measurement. The four exclusions were:

This narrows both target coverage and same-source coverage. Actual source paths give one same-source and nine different-source measured pairs. Frozen target-level intent tags were overinclusive: some different-source edges carried a same-source tag and all T03 edges carried a tradeoff tag. The tags are retained as design metadata, never accuracy labels. Their use in greedy control selection failed to secure a reversal of the actual tradeoff pair.

## What changes after consequences

| Transition | Family A | Family B |
|---|---:|---:|
| Stable overall relation | 9/10 | 9/10 |

| Dominance → tradeoff | 0/6 | 0/9 |

Family A's C006 weakens from assembly dominance to equality. Its rationale now treats the realized matrix as familiar collation whose principal exclusion was already available. Family B's C012 resolves tradeoff to probe dominance after the probe recovers a latent reading and the matrix leaves the principal assembly alternatives unresolved. There are no strengthened, changed-to-tradeoff or other transitions. Confidence changes are retained separately; they do not redefine these categories.

Consequences can legitimately change a displacement judgment: inquiry design and the warrant actually obtained are different objects. These two changes are compatible with that distinction, but neither is clean evidence of a causal consequence effect. C006 also changes under presentation reversal. C012 combines favorable probe informativeness, weak matrix discrimination and a Family B chronological over-inference. The nine stable relations per family suggest operation design often determines the broad comparison in this sample; they do not quantify how much of displacement is caused by operations versus signals.

C012 is the only dominance/tradeoff disagreement and the substantive tradeoff demonstration. Prospectively both families preserve the probe's deeper local intervention and the matrix's broader formation-hypothesis coverage without forcing a winner. Family A retains that distinction after the assay. Family B instead discounts the matrix's realized novelty. This is a centrality judgment, not a three-of-five vote. Tradeoff is not used as a catch-all, but one pair cannot establish its general reliability.

Local depth, inquiry propagation and task reach are distinguishable in the prose. The probe changes a local evidence channel deeply; its subsequent collation propagates that change; an assembly matrix covers more of the complete formation question. Yet the last distinction is poorly operationalized. Family A often judges task breadth balanced where Family B credits any materially altered reasoning with greater reach than an opponent characterized as native. They are sometimes comparing different things: scope of the task addressed versus scope of the *displaced* part. Native reducibility also remains a judgment about the stipulated practitioner, with only 6/10 agreement in each stage.

Comparative diagnostics avoid B.1's intrinsic low/moderate/high claims. They do not demonstrate removal of opponent anchoring. In T03, Family B credits the assembly matrix with broad novelty against custody but describes it as close to ordinary practice against the probe. A comparative advantage may legitimately depend on the opponent; a changing characterization of the same native baseline is more concerning. Single draws and one repeated triangle cannot separate framing from stochastic variability. The lost T05/T08 triangles further restrict this audit.

## Dominance graphs and presentation controls

Only dominance creates a directed edge. Tradeoff and equality create none; equality is not treated as transitive equivalence. No total sort or missing-edge inference is performed.

Only T01 and T03 retain complete preregistered triangles. Neither has a directed cycle in either family or stage. Family B supports inverse > matrix > diagnosis with its measured closure at T01 in both stages. In Stage B it also supports probe > assembly > custody with its closure at T03. Family A has no two-edge chain with a measured closure to test. No chain-closure tension is observed. Zero cycles in these two eligible targets cannot establish acyclicity across six measured or eight planned targets; single edges and broken triangles are uninformative.

Across primary edges, tradeoff density is 1/10 for each family prospectively, then 1/10 for A and 0/10 for B. Equality density is 3/10 versus 0/10, then 4/10 versus 0/10. In T03 specifically, tradeoff occupies one of three edges for both families prospectively and only A consequentially. T01 has one equality edge for A throughout; T03 adds one for A consequentially. These densities describe the sampled graphs, not population frequencies.

Controls reverse C001, C014, C006 and C008 as C002, C005, C009 and C011. Overall relations survive all four reversals per family in Stage A. In Stage B they survive 3/4 for A and 4/4 for B. **C006-A changes from equality to assembly dominance in reversed C009**, both close. Its action, decomposition, trajectory, local depth and propagation change too. This is genuine observed presentation/repeat sensitivity; with one draw per orientation it cannot be attributed uniquely to slot order.

Less dramatic field changes also matter. Stage A reversals change two criterion fields and one diagnostic for A, one criterion for B. Stage B changes three criterion fields and two diagnostics for A, two criterion fields and two diagnostics for B. Overall invariance therefore does not imply rationale invariance. Controls span four targets but omit C012 and the clear inversion/step/reduction comparisons. This limits claims about tradeoff stability and reflects the overinclusive tag-based selection described above.

## Evidence interpretation and outcome-authoring audit

[Primary outcome audits](../common-evidence-displacement-v0.3/review/outcome-audit.json) cover every measured pair. [Planned-pair audits](../common-evidence-displacement-v0.3/review/planned-outcome-audit.json) additionally cover all six excluded edges. Each checks authored informativeness, unjustified decisiveness, hidden assumptions, fact favoritism, resources and consistency. No undeclared world mutation, extra external source or ledger overrun was found. All consequences reproduce from frozen provenance. The following concerns remain substantive:

* **T01:** Additive truth, informative route geometry and zero realized noise favor inversion. Both alternative operations obtain useful evidence, but diagnosis records much less explicit analysis than the matrix despite allocating analytical work. C014-B relies on that absence. Equal budgets do not equalize recording completeness. Numerical conclusions also remain subject to the shared measurement bound: a nominal 2 ms route difference can arise within two endpoint errors of at most 1 ms each, so it does not alone require a nonzero residual contribution. The matrix's authored residual claim is stronger than this uncertainty permits; C014-B acknowledges marginality but still relies on the residual.
* **T02:** A complete-document hash authenticates BD, which the shared facts say contains exact K. Family B discounts this as document-only rather than clause evidence, while Family A recognizes the bridge. Separately, the authored clause outcome labels a memo *about* K as an occurrence even though the displayed text does not quote K. Both interpretation drift and overbroad outcome labeling are present despite equal reads, time and followup.
* **T03:** Positive assay recovery is materially stronger new information than a matrix reiterating an initially visible join constraint. This asymmetry follows the frozen world but still reflects author selection. Family B infers a day-4-to-day-12 revision window in C007/C012; a separate day-4 Q witness does not place Q on leaf A or lower-bound A's revision. Family A explicitly withholds that inference in C012. Direction agreement therefore does not validate the temporal reasoning.
* **T05:** The world deliberately contains an interaction useful to reduction. The 20-trial cap prevents completed minimization. Family A preserves the absence of a BD-alone sufficiency test; Family B overstates necessity within BDE and implications of passing other subsets. Monotonicity of a revision chain does not license arbitrary-subset monotonicity. Richer analytical narration also favors reduction over diagnosis.
* **T06:** Both branches yield the same dates from useful evidence. Every event referenced by a letter has a unique calendar occurrence. Family B invokes the calendar's repeated fair event to defend alignment's special warrant, although no letter references that event. This distinction is possible in another world but not exercised here.
* **T08:** Frozen potential-response arrays supply a transient for stepping and a largely flat plateau for monitoring. The coexistence of a baseline, adverse current snapshot, reset replicas, a zero-to-one step and holding current settings leaves the exact initial input state ambiguous. Family B reads the branches as beginning at different exposures. Hash identity cannot resolve this physical ambiguity. The result is retained with a material same-world qualification. Six hours also do not establish equilibrium or a completed intervention policy.
* **Excluded T04/T07:** Both alternatives have bounded, nontrivial outcomes, but admission rejects one side before comparison. Route localization and documentary corroboration do not themselves validate remedies; reversal counts remain small. Their absence is coverage loss, not a displacement finding.

The freeze order prevents adapting a world to provider judgments. It does not prove freedom from outcome rigging in the broader sense of author-selected worlds that favor future mappings. T03's informativeness asymmetry, T01/T05's analytical recording asymmetry, T02's labeling and T08's state ambiguity require prominent qualification. Operator-authored worlds are not ground truth and validity admission did not catch every comparative-design issue.

## Historical comparison and research conclusions

This comparison was made only after B.2 froze; [B.1](comparative-displacement-v0.3.md) remains byte-for-byte unchanged. B.1 had 22/28 overall agreement, 22/23 same direction among jointly directional pairs, one opposition and all six sampled reversals preserved per family. B.2's zero opposition occurs on only six then five jointly dominant pairs. Different admission, different common worlds, a changed relation schema and separate consequence access make an improvement claim unwarranted. B.2's one reversal change is an additional negative observation, not evidence of a population deterioration rate.

The common-evidence success question has a split answer. **Literal initial-context inequality was removed and auditability improved. Reduced interpretation instability was not demonstrated.** The complete-document hash, unsupported manuscript date, unexercised chronology ambiguity and rollout initial-state interpretation show persistent evidence-related drift. Aggregate agreement alone would obscure this.

Answers to the ten research questions are:

1. Common-start comparison is operationally feasible, with exact identity proofs and independently generated branches. Semantic world coherence still requires review beyond hashes.
2. Separating meaningfulness from direction makes the family equality threshold visible; it did not reduce that disagreement here.
3. Tradeoff captures a concrete local-depth versus task-reach conflict in C012. It is not synonymous with equality and was not indiscriminately used.
4. Observed jointly dominant directions are stable, but the small selected denominator and changed design do not establish greater stability than B.1.
5. Shared evidence removes a confound without eliminating interpretation drift; no causal reduction is demonstrated.
6. Generated signals support plausible stage changes. Authoring and presentation concerns prevent declaring both changes clean consequence effects.
7. Propagation within the investigation and reach across the task can be distinguished in rationales, but task-reach judgments remain especially incoherent across families.
8. Comparative diagnostics remove unsupported intrinsic scale claims. Opponent-related baseline framing remains, so reduced anchoring is unproven.
9. The two complete triangles have no cycles or closure tensions. Sparse graphs elsewhere provide no cycle evidence.
10. The representation is useful as a transparent, inspected Chaos Mage research control. It is insufficiently stable for autonomous creative-search selection or scalar ranking.

A partial-order representation is preferable to forcing every substantive difference into one winner or an ambiguous tie. It preserves the identified tradeoff and keeps meaningfulness distinct. This is a representational conclusion, not proof that the elicited relations satisfy a general mathematical partial order or that their epistemic interpretation is settled.

The recommended next research step is a bounded replication with independent review of baseline descriptions, common-state physics and consequence-record completeness before measurement. Vary frozen outcomes to include informative and uninformative operations; explicitly specify initial intervention settings; ensure comparable reporting of performed analysis; repeat the key tradeoff and equality contrasts in both orientations. Clarify task reach with concrete task components and distinguish tested evidence from hypothetical capabilities. This recommendation is **not executed**. Unresolved problems include family meaningfulness thresholds, centrality without a scalar rule, author-selected informativeness, opponent framing, task-reach semantics and sparse graph coverage.
