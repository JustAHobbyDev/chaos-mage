# Comparative ontological displacement v0.3 — Experiment B.1

2026-09-28. **Completed. Direct comparison adds useful resolution, but does not
establish greater reproducibility or a stable automatic ordering control. Recommend
retaining the comparative idea and revising/repeating B.1 before advancing.**

Across 28 primary conceptual pairs, the families agree on the overall relation in
22/28 cases. Among the 23 pairs where both choose a direction, 22 agree and one
reverses. The other five disagreements are direction versus tie. There are no
joint ties or indeterminate verdicts. All six reversed presentations preserve each
family's overall relation, including the opposed directions in the contested case.
Neither family produces a measured directed cycle.

The useful finding is concrete: comparison separates mappings with different
operations, signals, warrants and downstream consequences, including three pairs
that both Experiment B judges placed in the same absolute class. The limiting
finding is equally concrete: all three added resolutions are reviewed as close-order;
the families disagree about meaningful ties; the local/global tradeoff remains
unsettled; and the same mapping receives changing scope and counterfactual judgments
against different opponents. In one case a counterfactual flips from unlikely to
likely despite unchanged mapping, evidence and baseline bytes. No original judgment
was changed, reconciled, repaired or replaced. No grounding or retrieval work ran.

## The opposing-direction case: C008, repeated as C018

C008 compares a localized manuscript undertext probe (M019) with an explicit
competing-assembly-hypotheses matrix (M024). **A chooses the probe; B chooses the
matrix; both mark close.** The same normalized opposition persists in C018 after
reversing presentation. It is counted once in the primary denominator.

Both families agree that the probe changes action and observables more, that its
local displacement is high, and that its global propagation is low. Its reaction
supports presence/location of responsive material; independent collation and dated
correspondence, already supplied, license the local chronology. The full assembly
sequence remains unresolved.

A treats the matrix as largely ordinary comparative manuscript reasoning. It favors
the irreducible new signal and detection warrant even though their reach is bounded.
B treats the matrix as replacing best-fit corroboration with elimination, sensitivity
checks, surviving hypotheses and a changed stopping condition across the core target.
It favors that broader change over the probe's sharper local departure.

Two issues are inseparable here: how to weigh local depth against broad reach, and
how non-ordinary the matrix is in the first place. B's characterization of the baseline
as chiefly visual/corroborative is not fully specified by the actual baseline; A's
assumption that eliminative comparison is ordinary is also not independently verified.
The probe's documentary bridge may already support the same sequence constraint,
which limits its incremental role in the target claim. The labels make disagreement
inspectable but supply no principled common resolution. This is the strongest negative
finding for a single context-independent ordering, and it is not hidden by 22 agreeing directions.

## Shortcut and rationale audit

- **Vocabulary and source distance:** ordinary rule-removal language exceeds clinical
  diagnostic/rechallenge mappings in C010/C019 for its minimality warrant. The three
  same-source comparisons all produce concordant directions. These observations oppose
  an intrinsic source-distance explanation, but no name ablation or factorial design
  isolates source-label bias. The study cannot establish whether discipline predicts
  ordering better than operational criteria in a broader population.
- **Formalization versus changed warrant:** matrices repeatedly receive greater
  displacement than corroboration or diagnosis. Some reasons are operational—explicit
  exclusion and sensitivity checks—but B sometimes invents a baseline of best-fit
  support or unranked failure lists. The existing baselines already include comparison,
  disconfirmation, testing alternatives or bounded conclusions. This is a plausible
  shortcut toward favoring more explicit analytic structure, not a settled new warrant.
- **Evidence context:** judges generally name and preserve the separate contexts.
  They also repeatedly acknowledge that endpoint-only access, a validated oracle,
  marked uncertain records, or a controllable sandbox enables the claimed departure.
  Their assertion that the direction would survive equal access is not measured.
  No indeterminate verdict establishes that the supplied contexts are causally
  interchangeable. Feasibility and task breadth remain confounds.
- **Baseline additions:** C017-B broadens the baseline's exclusion of quantitative
  alignment of long measured series into non-ordinary systematic sequence alignment,
  despite ordinary pattern comparison being explicit. C008/C018-B treat manuscript
  reasoning as corroborative best-fit; C028-B adds a strong contrast with ordinary
  unranked lists. These interpretive additions are retained as concerns, not corrected.
- **Scope and applicability:** judges can represent high local/low global displacement,
  but global ratings still mix procedural propagation, the breadth of supplied evidence,
  and generalization beyond a testbed. The C008 opposition and repeated scope changes
  show that adding fields has not settled the concept.
- **Criterion counting:** no numerical composite or explicit majority rule is present.
  Rationales often mention all five advantages but identify a central causal link.
  C007-A orders on one trajectory advantage with four ties, a particularly useful
  demonstration that criterion counts do not mechanically determine the verdict.
- **Inference strength and overstatement:** the stronger direct remedy test does not
  automatically beat inverse localization. However, B occasionally describes reduction
  or diagnosis as supporting necessity more broadly than the mapping allows. Interpret
  these outputs as bounded replay minimality or narrowing plausible explanations,
  never population-wide necessary features or a unique causal mechanism.
- **Grounding and performance:** no grounding fields, usefulness score or retrieval
  control was introduced. Synthetic validity stipulations are not real-world grounding
  evidence. Neither successful attribution nor greater usefulness is established by
  a displacement preference.

The core directional rationales are inspectable and generally support their stated
preferences under their own baseline interpretations. That is weaker than agreement
on the interpretation itself. The contested and order-versus-tie cases, plus the
diagnostic reversals, remain negative evidence rather than being averaged away.

## Answers to the research questions and recommendation

1. **Direct comparison without classes:** yes, both families produced complete
   structured comparisons without being shown absolute labels or old judgments.
   Their unobservable internal reasoning cannot be established from outputs.
2. **Greater stability than B:** not demonstrated. Jointly directional relations
   seldom reverse, but common-set exact agreement is lower and tie interpretation
   differs sharply. The study adds resolution rather than establishing superiority
   in reproducibility.
3. **Resolution within old ties:** yes, three concordant directions among seven
   pairs tied by both old classifiers, all reviewed as close-order; one further
   resolution applies to B's historical tie alone.
4. **Operational criteria versus vocabulary:** the rationales and same-source
   contrasts favor operational explanations. Warrant is frequently decisive but
   not the most agreed criterion; decomposition also contributes real resolution.
5. **Does local/global separation reduce ambiguity?** It makes ambiguity explicit
   and handles the stipulated narrow/broad step-response case. It does not settle
   the tradeoff in C008 or the scope of “global.”
6. **Decisive-basis agreement:** exact primary-criterion agreement is 17/28.
   Compatible linked action/signal/warrant reasoning explains additional concordant
   directions, while the disputed cases turn on different baseline interpretations.
7. **Enough transitivity for control?** The measured graph has no directed cycles,
   which is encouraging. Sparse coverage, a tie-substitution tension, and unstable
   mapping diagnostics preclude treating it as a validated sortable total order.
8. **Presentation order:** no overall changes in six controls per family, but
   several diagnostic changes. Wider invariance and a causal order effect are untested.
9. **Source/vocabulary bias:** no simple exotic-name explanation accounts for the
   highlighted operational contrasts. Bias is not causally ruled out; explicitness
   and source-associated procedure may still influence the judgments.
10. **Continue developing comparative displacement?** Yes, as an inspected research
    description of concrete departures from ordinary inquiry. Revise/repeat B.1
    before relying on it for unattended candidate selection or advancing this research
    sequence on an assumption that comparative ordering is established.

The answer to the experiment's central question is therefore qualified: direct
comparison provides coherent operational resolution that could help a researcher
prefer mappings which change evidence and inquiry rather than merely sound foreign.
It does not yet provide sufficiently stable meaning for the tie threshold, global
scope, or baseline-relative diagnostics to function as an automatic retrieval signal.

The next research design should distinguish propagation *within the stipulated
investigation* from applicability to the full target task; assess mapping-specific
counterfactuals independently of the opponent; test coherent common-evidence states
with renewed validity admission; and clarify when formalization changes a warrant
rather than merely making ordinary reasoning explicit. Repeats are needed to separate
opponent effects from sampling variation and to test meaningful ties. These are
recommendations, not executed amendments. Do not revise frozen B or B.1 to fit them.

Limitations include one primary draw per family/case, six non-independent reversal
controls, purposive synthetic authorship, only eight unverified baselines, 12 repeated
instruments, favorable validity stipulations, correlated pair occurrences, differing
evidence contexts, opaque backend snapshots, and researcher-authored rather than
independent practitioner review. No success percentage or adjudicated ground truth
was defined. The authorization ends with B.1 verification, publication and push;
**grounding Experiment C and retrieval integration remain unexecuted.**
