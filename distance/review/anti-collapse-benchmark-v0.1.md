# Experiment D — Anti-collapse gate benchmark v0.1

Completed 2026-09-29. **The gate shows the intended permissive separation on this
admitted authored benchmark: both families reject all five terminology-only designs,
retain all twelve C/D designs, and keep the uncertain manuscript-probe case.** A
single mandatory evidence-selection rule is enough for sufficient departure even
when evidence type, interpretation and decomposition remain ordinary. This supports
moving the frozen gate into a subsequent research test on naturally generated
Chaos Mage mappings, not deploying it into generation or retrieval behavior.

The finding is bounded by strong authoring cues, synthetic operational stipulations,
unverified practitioner baselines and validity attrition that removes half the B
formalization designs. Perfect status agreement is not proof of correctness, broad
reliability, independent locus calibration or freedom from prompt cues. No operator
intention is treated as ground truth. No measurement was repaired or repeated.

## Baselines, all 24 designs and admission

Six historical task/practitioner baselines were frozen before source selection and
mapping authorship. Every inherited field is unchanged. The two added fields make
ordinary inference and next actions explicit without changing scope. Exact baseline
file bytes occur in every same-target judge packet. Facts and available resources
are shared across each ladder, and absent access does not redefine native practice.

The software baseline already includes rollback and controlled reintroduction, so
those operations remain native; the C/D designs instead use joint route constraints.
This implements the user's decision that native baselines constrain case design,
never the reverse. All baselines remain scoped authored hypotheses, not verified
practitioner consensus. No grounding research was performed.

comparison is an authoring weakness: it can obscure an essential source chain even
when the intended change was only recording format.

The primary cohort contains **20 cases across all six targets: 5 A, 3 B, 6 C and
6 D**. All five intended C loci survive. Every preregistered viability condition
passes. Only T01–T03 retain complete ladders. T04 lacks A/B; T05 and T06 lack B.
Consequently only three B/C contrasts survive, while all six C/D contrasts survive.
The formalization boundary and organizational native-collapse floor are less tested
than the 24-case design intended. No denominator includes an excluded mapping.

## Neutralization and its audit

Source-framed mappings and neutral procedures were co-authored before measurement
using shared operational sentences. Source framing is a removable imagery prefix;
source identity and generic source descriptions are excluded from anti-collapse
packets. Neutral files retain state/procedure, action, observables, warrant,
decomposition, next inquiry, limits and stopping conditions. The judge never generates
or repairs a paraphrase.

The frozen [neutralization audit](../anti-collapse-v0.1/neutralization-audit.json)
checks removal of framing and preservation of operations, signals, warrants,
conditions/stopping rules and next inquiry for all 24 cases. It also records
preservation of decomposition. All checks pass, and executable correspondence checks
verify every operational sentence across the two forms. No paraphrase was edited
after provider calls began.

This establishes strong textual correspondence, not independent semantic validation.
The same implementation operator authored cases, audits and review. Several procedures
explicitly describe reasoning as ordinary, deny that a matrix adds a warrant, or say
which units and follow-up remain unchanged. These operational disclaimers can also cue
the intended contrast. No wording ablation tested whether judgments depend on them.

## A/B behavior and adversaries

Both families reject **5/5 admitted A designs**: clinical-style debugging, reporting
corroboration, ordinary manuscript comparison, proposal variant testing and archival
event-pattern matching. A-level retention is **0/5 in each family**. Their rationales
identify lossless replacement by the supplied native method, rather than rejecting
source fidelity or rewarding foreign discipline.

Both reject **3/3 admitted B designs**: candidate/test matrix, claim/source matrix,
and anomaly catalogue. The shared reason is unchanged evidence, warrant and next-step
logic. Formal layout alone is not treated as a new epistemic constraint. However,
these surviving B descriptions are explicit bookkeeping controls. There is **no
B-level disagreement or borderline verdict**; the hardest formalization boundary is
under-exercised after three B exclusions. The run therefore does not demonstrate how
a borderline B would fare.

All six planned A designs carried exotic-framing tags; five survive admission and
all five collapse after neutralization. All twelve mundane-operational C/D designs
survive admission and are kept by both families. Plain procedure language suffices
for retention. Since framing was removed and there was **no source-framing control
arm**, this demonstrates behavior on neutralized adversaries, not a causal estimate
of terminology bias or robustness when foreign terminology is visible.

zero suppression cases among twelve C/D cases and zero rejecting outcomes among their
24 judgments. This is a diagnostic count against design coverage, not an error rate
against ground truth.

D designs. The C cases make the central distinction concrete:

N002 is especially informative. With the same finite response dictionary and one
request, the worst-case survivor-count rule selects R3 rather than the stipulated
native discretionary R1. Both judges mark action and next inquiry present while
warrant and decomposition remain absent. A also marks observables absent; B uncertain.
They identify a binding evidence-acquisition constraint, not a requirement for several
independent innovations. The fixture favorably stipulates R1 as the native choice and
R3 as fully discriminating within the retained accounts. Real reporters' choices and
out-of-dictionary responses are not measured.

N004 exercises the permissive branch. Both find action uncertain and leave materiality,
lossless reduction and mere formalization uncertain. A observes that invisibility alone
does not make evidence non-native; B sees a new observable but remains uncertain about
materiality. Neither rejects simply because the assay may be an uncommon native
variation. This is evidence that uncertainty can protect a valid local intervention,
not evidence that the intervention is definitely foreign to manuscript practice.

N020's coordinated prediction/control procedure is SD/SD. N007/N019 coordinate bounded
fitting with residuals and selected observations; N017 coordinates reduction and
minimality checks; N009 coordinates globally consistent offsets/gaps. These D cases
are retained without requiring every locus to be present. N024 has a special mechanism
disagreement discussed below.

Primary-locus coverage is **not pure single-field isolation**. One operational rule
can change actions, derived observations and warrants together. All eleven SD cases
in each family have action marked present. This corpus therefore cannot rule out an
action-centric shortcut or establish acceptance of a departure whose action field is
absent. The strongest supported claim concerns one independent rule, not one and only
one `present` field.

## Native reduction, formalization and matched ladders

Each family reports exactly eight lossless reductions (`yes`), eleven material losses
(`no`), and one uncertain reduction. Materiality and mere-formalization statuses follow
the corresponding pattern. For collapse cases, lost-if-reduced lists are empty. For
retained cases, the named losses include compatible-set attribution, required record
selection, a controlled material response, local minimality conditions and joint
chronology constraints. The reduction test produces inspectable reasons rather than
a numeric novelty degree.

The gate distinguishes formal expressions from binding constraints: a matrix is
removable when it only stores ordinary comparisons; a selection, retention, stopping
or evidence-acceptance rule can remain material after its technical name is removed.
Ordinary follow-up does not force rejection of a changed local operation. These are
substantive observations beyond the aggregate agreement.

There are no observed retained-lower/rejected-higher ladder reversals and no
A=SD/C-or-D=CC pathology. The three complete ladders read:

- T01: CC → CC → SD → SD.
- T02: CC → CC → SD → SD.
- T03: CC → CC → BK → SD.

These are displayed statuses along authored contrasts, not a numerical ordering.
Missing A/B cases preclude the corresponding contrasts in the other three ladders.
No judgment was corrected to make a ladder monotonic.

**N024 is the strongest disagreement hidden by status agreement.** A finds the
mandatory discrimination-directed request sufficient, with alignment nativeness
uncertain and the final warrant native. B finds the sequence-alignment operation,
observable, warrant and decomposition sufficient, but leaves next inquiry uncertain
because “best discriminates” is underspecified. Four locus statuses differ. A
explicitly conditions R3 selection on all three accounts surviving; no alignment
outcome is supplied, so the actual next request cannot be asserted unconditionally.
Both identify a possible material loss, but they have not calibrated the same boundary.

Other disagreements concern whether a derived feasible set or deletion boundary is a
new observable, whether intermediate variables constitute a new decomposition, and
whether ordinary discrimination becomes a new next-step rule when its objects change.
The [all-case reviews](../anti-collapse-v0.1/review/case-reviews.json) preserve every
status and locus disagreement, including concordant cases.

## Interpretation, unresolved problems and recommendation

The benchmark answers the research question positively **within its admitted, authored
scope**: foreign-framed native restatements are rejected after neutralization, all valid
C/D designs are preserved, and an uncertain local intervention is kept. N002 supplies
an especially clear one-rule retention example. There is no observed requirement for
spectacular global reorganization, new raw measurements, or multiple independent
innovations. This looks permissive enough to justify another research test for a
generator. It is not a deployment finding.

Material unresolved issues are:

1. **Selection and coverage:** four validity exclusions include three B designs and
   both organizational native controls. The surviving B cases are unusually explicit
   formalization-only examples. Validity's interpretation of bounded target contribution
   remains contested, independently of anti-collapse.
2. **Author cues and favorable fixtures:** the same operator supplies baselines,
   synthetic bridges, ordinary/departure descriptions, neutralization and review.
   Statements that a table changes no warrant, and the stipulated R1/R3 comparison,
   make some contrasts easy. Natural-generation behavior remains unknown.
3. **Baseline interpretation:** B sometimes describes absence of an inverse-problem
   specialty as exclusion of inverse methods, and describes native chronology as a
   single dated account or merely per-document clues. The actual baselines preserve
   alternatives and do not settle every method. Fixed bytes do not eliminate drift.
4. **Availability versus nativeness:** B's N004 uncertainty partly invokes equal probe
   availability. Availability alone cannot settle whether the operation is ordinary.
   The final keep is appropriate to the uncertainty policy, but that reasoning warrants
   scrutiny in later samples.
5. **Locus dependence:** all sufficient cases have action present; five primary-locus
   designs do not establish five independently calibrated pathways. N024 also shows
   that exact status agreement can coexist with different decisive mechanisms.
6. **Inferential overstatement:** B's N020 language about eliminating accounts on
   mismatch must remain bounded by sensitivity and interference. Neither controls nor
   chronology establish an entire history. Judgments are evidence about the gate, not
   verification of all scientific claims in their rationales.
7. **Limited replication:** one observation per family/case, related ladders and repeated
   inverse instruments do not estimate within-family reliability or population error
   rates. Opaque served snapshots further limit reproducibility.
8. **Framing and neutralization:** no framed arm, automatic neutralizer, independent
   paraphrase audit or natural-output sample was tested. No causal immunity to source
   imagery is established.

**Recommendation: next test the frozen anti-collapse gate on naturally generated
Chaos Mage mappings**, with separately frozen native baselines and operator-authored,
audited neutralizations. Include naturally occurring formalization and modest local
changes so the next sample does not depend on explicit benchmark cues. Treat validity
attrition and any C-like suppression as primary diagnostic findings. Keep this as a
research measurement; do not integrate the gate into production behavior yet.

That recommendation is not executed. No grounding, comparative-distance continuation,
diversity filter, usefulness score, retrieval integration or natural-output testing
was performed. Original judgments, historical experiments and accepted instruments
remain unchanged.
