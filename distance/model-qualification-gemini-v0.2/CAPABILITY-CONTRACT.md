# Capability contract — v0.1

Frozen 2026-09-29 before any provider calls for this qualification.

The question is whether the candidate can competently instantiate Chaos Mage's
frozen reasoning protocols without systematic failure that makes it unsuitable
as one of multiple high-reasoning model implementations.

1. **Protocol compliance:** follow frozen instructions, canonical structured-output
   schemas, enums, required fields, exclusions, and fresh isolated contexts.
   Mechanical schema failure is a capability concern.
2. **Instrument structure:** distinguish state, operation, signal, inference, and
   limit. Operation is not signal; signal is not inference; inference is not an
   automatically licensed target conclusion.
3. **Mechanism preservation:** distinguish an operative transferred mechanism from
   foreign vocabulary pasted onto ordinary target reasoning.
4. **Evidential warrant:** distinguish an unobserved signal from a signal that,
   even if observed, would not license the claimed inference.
5. **Execution versus epistemic validity:** distinguish missing execution
   prerequisites from absent mechanism, warrant, or target-fidelity bridges.
   Do not pathologically reject all unexecuted prospective procedures. The existing
   v0.3 protocol applies; this does not introduce Experiment F's proposed taxonomy.
6. **Native-baseline respect:** use the frozen baseline without narrowing it to
   manufacture novelty, broadening it to collapse everything, or ignoring ordinary
   target methods explicitly supplied.
7. **Anti-collapse:** recognize native restatement and formalization-only change,
   while preserving modest material, strong coordinated, and uncertain plausible
   departures. Historical label reproduction is not required.
8. **Bounded uncertainty:** meaningfully use Conditional, BORDERLINE_KEEP, and
   uncertain; neither force certainty nor use uncertainty as a universal escape.
9. **Inspectable reasoning:** explain the mechanism, signal, licensed inference,
   relevant condition/limit, and gate decision through coherent structured rationale.

## Decision rule

**qualified:** reliable protocol/schema behavior and coherent reasoning across the
above dimensions, with no systematic disqualifying pathology. Defensible substantive
disagreement is acceptable.

**not_qualified:** a documented repeated pattern of model failure, such as unsupported
inference, lost mechanisms, baseline disregard, formalization-as-novelty, rejection
of modest departures, confusion of unobserved outcome with invalid inference,
schema failure, or internally contradictory rationale. One controversial case is
insufficient.

**qualification_inconclusive:** provider/schema failure, uncertain model identity,
insufficient valid measurements, or predominating protocol ambiguity prevents a
defensible capability judgment. Missing evidence is not model failure.

No agreement threshold, majority vote, historical accuracy metric, adjudicator,
prompt tuning, semantic repair, or post-result reruns. Record harness ambiguity and
recommend later specification revision; record model pathology and recommend a
separately authorized candidate qualification. Do not revise and rerun v0.1.
