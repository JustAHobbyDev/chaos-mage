# H6.R3 contracts

## TARGET_FIDELITY

Is this proposition explicitly supplied by the frozen target/problem material, or a faithful non-expansive restatement of it at the same scope and certainty?

## SOURCE_FIDELITY

Is this proposition explicitly contained in, or a faithful non-expansive restatement of, the frozen source instrument and its stated mechanism, conditions, or limits?

## DERIVED_WARRANT

Given the cited premises, observations, and transferred mechanism, does the claimed target conclusion follow at the strength and scope stated?

## CONDITIONAL_LICENSE

Under the stated conditions, does the transferred mechanism license the claimed relation between each hypothetical outcome and its stated evidential or operational consequence, without assuming that the outcome has actually occurred?

## OPERATION_LICENSE

Does this proposed observation, intervention, comparison, or transformation faithfully instantiate the transferred mechanism at the target, under the stated applicability conditions, and does it produce information or action relevant to the target problem?

## LIMIT_WARRANT

Does the cited mechanism, evidence, or target structure justify the stated limitation, ambiguity, confound, scope restriction, or failure condition at the strength stated?

## FACT_WARRANT

Is this mapping-generated descriptive assertion supported by the cited observations or premises at the scope and certainty stated, without importing an unstated substantive inference?

## GOVERNANCE_STRUCTURE

Is this proposition genuinely a prospective decision rule with a sufficiently determinate trigger and a sufficiently determinate prescribed response, and is the decision structure internally coherent at the stated scope? Judge only the rule's decision structure. Do not evaluate whether an embedded operation is informative, mechanism-licensed, effective, causally appropriate, or target-relevant, and do not evaluate the truth or warrant of embedded factual, inferential, conditional, or limiting premises; those belong exclusively to their separate obligations.

## Mandatory governance boundary

Do not judge the scientific, causal, diagnostic, informational,
mechanistic, empirical, or target relevance of embedded substantive
content.

Assume those contents receive their own independent evaluations.

Judge only whether the proposition forms a prospective, executable,
internally coherent decision rule at its stated scope.

A structural failure includes absent trigger, indeterminate response, incompatible responses without precedence, explicit nonterminating resolution loop, or contradictory policy. Irrelevant diagnostics, mechanism violations, false empirical reasons, unsupported thresholds, and overreaching outcome relations belong exclusively to secondary contracts. Preserve and flag prohibited substantive reasoning; never repair a judgment.
