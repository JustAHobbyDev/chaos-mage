# Core-invalidity threshold — frozen before case access

An unsupported inference is **core-invalidating** only when removing that
inference destroys the transferred procedure's distinctive material epistemic
contribution to the target. Any unsupported statement alone is insufficient.

These artifact statuses apply only after an actual warrant defect is identified.
They are not novelty scores. Delete all identified unsupported claims
simultaneously. Do not repair, replace, reinterpret into a better claim, or add
operations, measurements, hypotheses, or evidence. Remaining clauses retain
their original explicit limits and antecedent context; deleted content cannot
supply a premise. Grammatical debris is not itself mechanism failure.

## Structural tests

1. Mechanism survival: after deletion, does a coherent source-derived
   state → operation → signal → supported-inference chain remain? Absence of a
   distinctive operational mechanism favors CORE_INVALID.
2. Target contribution survival: does the remainder materially contribute to
   the target? A discriminating observation, useful intervention, hypothesis
   elimination, different problem decomposition, stopping rule, material next
   inquiry, or bounded causal/historical constraint can suffice. A complete
   target answer is unnecessary. Wholesale rejection despite a surviving
   material contribution requires additional justification.
3. Dependency cascade: trace signal → defective inference → dependent claims
   → dependent actions/next inquiries. Distinguish leaf overclaims from premises
   supporting the investigation. Do not equate the number of defects with depth.

## Decision rule and strict derivation

- KEEP_WITH_WARRANT_FLAGS: mechanism survives AND target contribution
  substantially_survives; essentially the same material contribution remains
  and no major advertised branch depends on the deleted claims. Keep and flag;
  defective claims are not licensed.
- KEEP_WITH_REDUCED_SCOPE: mechanism survives or partially_survives AND target
  contribution reduced_but_material. A meaningful advertised branch is lost,
  but another distinctive coherent useful branch survives. State the reduced
  contribution without inventing a replacement method.
- CORE_INVALID: mechanism does_not_survive OR target contribution
  no_material_contribution. All useful distinctive contribution is lost, all
  material contributions depend on the defect, or only generic non-contributory
  activity survives. A total dependency cascade must be represented in at least
  one of those structural statuses; prose alone cannot bypass this invariant.
  Reject/quarantine the transfer as currently mapped.
- UNCERTAIN_LOAD_BEARING: at least one relevant structural dimension is
  uncertain and necessity is not established. Keep and flag uncertainty;
  false rejection is more costly than uncertain retention.

Record surviving contributions and failed/surviving downstream claims/actions
with exact mapping anchors. Describe only weaker contributions already licensed
by the remainder. No combinatorial subsets in v0.1.
