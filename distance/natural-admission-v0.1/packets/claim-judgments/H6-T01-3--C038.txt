execution prerequisites are satisfied and the described signal occurs. Does that
operation/signal license this specific claim within its stated limits?

CONDITIONAL_WARRANT: the mapping itself supplies a specific unresolved empirical
relation whose establishment would license it. Describe and cite that relation;
do not invent it or confuse execution readiness with an inferential warrant.

do not silently judge a corrected version. Do not judge other claims or whole-artifact
viability. No repair, replacement inference, or quality/creativity ranking.
