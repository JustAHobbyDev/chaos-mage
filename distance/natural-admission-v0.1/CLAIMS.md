# Freeze atomic warrant-bearing claims

Enumerate the mapping's warrant-bearing semantic claims across all five fields.
Use C001 onward. Split independently contestable conclusions, distinctions, bounds,
conditional outcome relations and proposed-operation rationales into atomic claims.
Keep each claim's conditions and limits; an if/then relation is not an unconditional
assertion. Do not explode every noun or routine instruction into an independent claim.
Do not assign warrant status or artifact status. Do not improve or complete the mapping.
No fixed claim count, pruning by importance, centrality, novelty or expected validity.

A claim is a frozen semantic annotation with H.5 provenance, not a new source span.
Several claims may cite the same coarse span. Capture every material proposition used
by the transfer, including stated assumptions and limitations; coverage records explain
non-warrant-bearing setup in each of the five fields. Duplicate formulations of one
proposition are one claim with all relevant support. Each claim's fields list gives
source locations, not ownership rights over cited spans. Record ambiguity explicitly.
