# Frozen natural-output review rubric

Review only after all available scientific judgments freeze. One operator audit per
mapping; this is diagnostic review, not a duplicate model judgment or adjudication.
Do not overwrite outputs, change statuses, repair citations, retry, select alternative
ablations or select favorable examples. Record evidence with packet/component IDs,
frozen paths and an explanation. Review all 18, including clean cases. Missing categories
are reported as absent, never manufactured. No usefulness or creativity ratings.

## Audit sequence

1. Check immutable generation and mapping identity; verify no authored defects and no
   generation context received evaluation information. Count parsing/provider failures.
2. Compare atoms with all five original fields. Look for omitted claims, lost conditions,
   duplicated claims or semantic strengthening. Record limitations of semantic ablation.
3. Inspect each claim judgment in its frozen scope. A debatable judgment is retained.
   Distinguish execution prerequisites from the empirical bridge needed for warrant.
4. Verify all and only UNSUPPORTED atom IDs were deleted simultaneously and survivor
   objects are byte-equivalent under canonical JSON. Check no deleted premise reappears
   through shared spans, CONTEXT, paraphrases, candidate synthesis or outcome relations.
5. Check each inventory candidate's five dimensions in order. An important warning or
   insufficiency-only statement cannot become productive by assertion. Reconstruct
   target/inquiry counterfactual changes and inspect bounds and stopping conditions.
6. Scan all five fields for inquiry units without polarity filters. Check two live
   alternatives, selected specific operation, two differential outcome relations and
   complete surviving provenance. All complete units must have exactly one inquiry role.
7. Inspect every citation bundle closed: consider cited spans first, then compare full
   mapping to find omitted substantive premises or qualifiers. Extra context is not
   failure by itself. Record whether a correct bundle exists, without editing the output.
8. Inspect core/uncertain cases individually. Viability must control status, not graph
   centrality, ownership, surviving volume or mere provenance errors. Inspect reduced
   scope claims for an actual lost branch and KEEP cases for retained source machinery.
9. Check instrumentation signals and collision conditions; separately report measurements
   and this diagnostic audit. No latent collision exposure is inferred from file existence.

## Anomaly categories

| Category | Evidence threshold |
| --- | --- |
| GENERATOR_DEFECT | Original mapping asserts an unwarranted relation, invents a target fact, loses a source limit, or supplies unusable operative content. A cleanly traceable unsupported claim belongs here, not instrumentation failure. |
| WARRANT_JUDGE_ERROR | Specific frozen claim/signal/limit contradicts the recorded warrant rationale. A plausible alternative alone does not prove error. |
| REMAINDER_JUDGE_ERROR | Invented survivor, deletion leak, insufficiency rescue, inquiry coverage violation, incorrect viability/status rule, or scope loss inconsistent with the frozen inventory. |
| PROVENANCE_MODEL_ERROR | Wrong/missing citation, qualifier omission, role misuse or atomization misattribution where a correct H.5 bundle remains representable. |
| H5_INSTRUMENTATION_WEAKNESS | Competent inspection finds a genuinely supported component cannot be represented cleanly with the available spans and roles, or one of the eight concrete H.5 signals has actual evidence. |
| PACKET_COLLISION_FAILURE | Exact COLLISIONS.md trigger; report all conditions with evidence. |
| CASE_AMBIGUITY | Supplied mapping/target genuinely leaves a consequential interpretation unresolved. |
| PLAUSIBLE_ALTERNATIVE | Another coherent interpretation exists without establishing an error in the frozen judgment. |

Multiple categories can describe distinct issues. Each record has category, component,
evidence, explanation, severity, H.5 signal codes and correct_support_representable.
Uncertain diagnoses stay uncertain. A model-reported anomaly is evidence to inspect,
not automatically an architecture finding. Missing semantic review is not CLEAN.

## Instrumentation outcome (separate from scientific status)

CLEAN: no observed material citation or representation concern after review. Coarse
spans and deterministic ambiguity diagnostics alone do not prevent CLEAN.
WARNING: inspectable annotation errors, doubtful bundles, potential H.5 weakness,
atomization/deletion ambiguity or other limited measurement concern whose impact is
localized. Describe what can and cannot be concluded.
FAILURE: observed H.5 defect/collision or other instrumentation defect materially
prevents an interpretable measurement. Preserve scientific_status as observed and mark
its evidential limitation. Never convert it silently to CORE_INVALID.

## Eight H.5 signals (possible weakness, not automatic redesign)

1. H5_UNREPRESENTABLE_SUPPORTED_COMPONENT: supported component lacks clean representation.
2. H5_UNCITED_SUBSTANTIVE_DEPENDENCE: repeated need for uncited substantive content.
3. H5_COARSE_CONTRADICTION_OR_QUALIFIER: coarse span cannot distinguish material conflict.
4. H5_DISPERSED_SIMPLE_SUPPORT: simple component needs most of mapping for ordinary support.
5. H5_OUTSIDE_QUALIFIER: necessary qualifier is outside the cited bundle.
6. H5_CONTEXT_INFERENCE: CONTEXT does substantive inference, not interpretation.
7. H5_NONDETERMINISTIC_SEGMENTATION: identical content produces different spans.
8. H5_MARKUP_SCOPE_DAMAGE: natural markup splitting changes scope, negation or reference.

For 2/4/5/6, first ask whether proper citations/roles using current spans resolve it.
If yes, classify annotation/model error and report signal exposure separately from a
confirmed H.5 architecture failure. No clause splitting or packet-ID redesign in H.6.
