Use the frozen remainder inventory without rejudging claims or inventing candidates.
A definite viable remainder requires all five ordered viability dimensions YES.
A potentially decisive unresolved candidate has no dimension NO and at least one
UNCERTAIN. Do not turn instrumentation errors into scientific invalidity.

KEEP_WITH_WARRANT_FLAGS: essentially the same material distinctive source-derived
transfer survives, with any local/conditional/uncertain flags. This is also the status
when no local defect was found (the vocabulary has no unqualified KEEP).
KEEP_WITH_REDUCED_SCOPE: a meaningful branch/scope was lost, but a narrower definite
viable contribution survives.
CORE_INVALID: no definite viable remainder and no decisive viability issue unresolved.
UNCERTAIN_LOAD_BEARING: no definite viable remainder, but a potentially decisive
survivor remains genuinely unresolved.

With definite survivors, decide whether loss is meaningful to the transfer's scope,
not by number of deleted claims or amount of text. If uncertain about scope loss,
retain permissively with flags and disclose that uncertainty. No usefulness, creativity,
distance, quality or overall merit ranking. Never invoke ownership or graph centrality.
Explain every core failure through absence of viable source-derived remainder.
