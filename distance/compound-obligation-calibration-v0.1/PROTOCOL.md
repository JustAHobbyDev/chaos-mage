# H6.R2 protocol v0.1

Bounded synthetic calibration only. Exactly twelve cases, thirteen claim records
(P3 has two), no replacement or expansion after Stage A begins. No H.6 admission,
ablation, retrospective relabeling, terminal-state precedence fix, provider
no-observation formalization, natural-output run or main merge is authorized.

The controlling definitions are CLASSIFICATION.md, OBLIGATIONS.md, CONTRACTS.md,
taxonomy.json and schemas. Hidden operator hypotheses are frozen before calls;
they are comparison hypotheses, not provider-visible answers or established truth.
Cases use neutral public IDs; case-control labels stay in hidden hypotheses.
P3 mapping context explicitly states that C2's threshold choice relies on C1.

Stage A: fresh session per claim, ordered atomicity/origin/function assignment;
freeze all thirteen before Stage B. Stage B: fresh session per eligible claim,
with frozen classification and same-case frozen claim inventory; discover base/direct
obligations, references and closure plus embedded-subject functions. Freeze all sets
before evaluation. Stage C: fresh isolated session for every unique base/inline
obligation. No model call for CLAIM_REF; reuse its single independently evaluated
claim. Freeze all responses. Stage D: mechanical DAG aggregation; no provider calls.
Then and only then compare hidden hypotheses and freeze operator audit.

Scientific errors remain observations: omission, wrong contract and unexpected
classification are reported, never repaired. Strict wire/schema failures pause.
Structurally invalid graphs cannot produce scientific aggregates. Missing required
base contracts remain omissions and prevent clean completion of the affected claim.
An omitted secondary may leave the observed obligation-set aggregate SATISFIED;
report that result prominently as potential GOVERNANCE_LAUNDERING, never silently
insert a hypothesis-derived judgment. Scientific control failure does not authorize
new samples or case reconstruction.

Provider policy: gpt-6-astra, high reasoning, serial manifest order, at most three
sessions per batch, zero retries/substitutions/probes/duplicate samples. Empty
isolated cwd, ignored rules/config, disabled tools/integrations. Record CLI/native
hashes, actual exposed identifiers and unobservable serving/internal retry limits.
Any provider capacity/transport event pauses and preserves state; do not retry.
Malformed responses pause too. Recovery follows docs/EXPERIMENT-RECOVERY-POLICY.md
and requires positive non-contamination evidence; no model observation is repaired.

Budget: one stable experiment ID/shared ledger. Before the first call present all
stages cumulatively: A=13, B<=13, C unknown until discovery. Unknown costs/fan-out
require approval. Resolve exact C count and reforecast before evaluation. Reserve
immediately before EVERY launch. Failed/uncertain launches retain reservations.
Fresh usage forecast before stages and bounded batches; reserve 10 percentage points.
Unknown/BORDERLINE/HIGH forecasts require real user review. Financial consent alone
does not cover usage. Keep account snapshots, consent and calibration under .runtime/.
Collect before/after observations and actual sessions/tokens around authorized work;
exclude confounded/reset-crossing/saturated samples; no extra calibration calls.

Freeze order: classification schema; routing; compound schema/propagation; cases and
hidden hypotheses; A packets; cumulative financial/usage preflight and authorization;
A judgments; B packets; B judgments; C packets; C judgments; aggregation; operator
audit; completion. Commit checkpoints and previous SHAs, verify before every launch.
All base-tracked files remain byte-identical. Push only the named H6.R2 branch.

Primary outcomes are exact structural checks N1-N6/P1-P3/N7/N8/U1. Report every
required diagnostic, exclusions and denominators, not just aggregate accuracy.
Schema representability is not measured reliability. Single judgments and synthetic
controls do not estimate natural-claim prevalence or justify H.6 continuation.
