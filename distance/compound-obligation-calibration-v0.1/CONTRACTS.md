# Evaluation contracts v0.1

## TARGET_FIDELITY

Is this proposition explicitly supplied by the frozen target/problem material, or a faithful non-expansive restatement of it at the same scope and certainty?

## SOURCE_FIDELITY

Is this proposition explicitly contained in, or a faithful non-expansive restatement of, the frozen source instrument and its stated mechanism, conditions, or limits?

## DERIVED_WARRANT

Given the cited premises, observations, and transferred mechanism, does the claimed target conclusion follow at the strength and scope stated?

## CONDITIONAL_LICENSE

Under the stated conditions, does the transferred mechanism license the claimed relation between each hypothetical outcome and its stated evidential or operational consequence, without assuming that the outcome has actually occurred?

## OPERATION_LICENSE

Does this proposed observation, intervention, comparison, or transformation faithfully instantiate the transferred mechanism at the target, under the stated applicability conditions, and does it produce information or action relevant to the target problem?

## GOVERNANCE_COHERENCE

Is this proposition genuinely presented as a prospective decision, safety, stopping, rollback, threshold, or policy rule; are its trigger and prescribed action coherent; and does it avoid falsely presenting an authored normative choice as an empirically established fact?

## LIMIT_WARRANT

Does the cited mechanism, evidence, or target structure justify the stated limitation, ambiguity, confound, scope restriction, or failure condition at the strength stated?

## FACT_WARRANT

Is this mapping-generated descriptive assertion supported by the cited observations or premises at the scope and certainty stated, without importing an unstated substantive inference?

## Routing and verdicts

Supplied origins route to target fidelity, source fidelity, or both, independent
of function. Generated origins route by function per taxonomy.json.
Every required contract has an isolated judgment: SATISFIED, CONDITIONAL,
VIOLATED, UNCERTAIN. CONDITIONAL records explicit unresolved conditions.
No universal SUPPORTED/UNSUPPORTED judgments are permitted.

Fidelity packets contain the claim plus only the corresponding frozen input.
Other contract packets contain the exact subject, whole-claim context, frozen target,
source mechanism/applicability and cited mapping context. Mapping assertions are not
observations; CONTEXT is not substantive SUPPORT. No expected verdicts, discovery
rationales, other evaluation results, or operator audit enter these packets.
Governance evaluation uses its unchanged contract; secondary evaluations are separate,
not a reason to instruct governance judges to ignore an actual coherence defect.
