# H6.R1 protocol — frozen before measurement

## Question and boundary

Can the seven-role taxonomy separate natural H.6 propositions and route each to
its own epistemic contract while retaining strict warrant for derived inferences?
This is a taxonomy calibration, not a rescue or reinterpretation of H.6. The
schema, definitions, questions, routing and verdict meanings in ROLE-SCHEMA.md,
CONTRACTS.md and taxonomy.json are controlling and frozen prospectively.

## Corpus and blindness

Use only frozen H.6 claim objects. Include all eleven handoff anchors. Select
additional cases from content without reading historical warrant judgments; the
user confirmed this priority over guaranteeing historical-status control balance.
Target 21, maximum 24. Aim for three natural cases per role, but retain shortages
and source/limit or conditional/inference ambiguity as findings. Do not invent
synthetic measured cases or replace claims following judgments. Offline engineering
fixtures never enter scientific denominators. Record hidden intended atomicity,
role and rationale before provider work. Operator hypotheses are not ground truth.

Provider-visible data are allowlisted claim value/scope, original citation refs,
frozen target/source context and neutral mapping spans. No old warrant label,
atomizer sufficiency assessment/rationale, operator hypothesis, selection reason,
or terminal interpretation is permitted. Original H.5 refs distinguish SUPPORT
from CONTEXT; context cannot supply missing substantive support. Generic prose in
the frozen source/mapping is evidence, not an old evaluation label.

## Stage A

One fresh isolated Astra/high judgment for each selected claim. Ask only for
atomicity, assignment status, primary role, competing roles, role provenance and
rationale (plus immutable claim ID). Do not ask about validity or show contracts.
Assign a role from the proposition's function and provenance, not its field name.
A mixed historical claim remains whole and can be ATOMIZATION_DEFECT.
Freeze all original responses, validated records and diagnostics before Stage B.
No operator repair or post-evaluation reclassification is allowed.

## Stage B

Only ATOMIC + ASSIGNED continues. Fresh isolated Astra/high judgment with the
frozen role and exactly its contract; do not include Stage A reasoning. Packets
use these evidence selections, fixed before outcomes:

- SUPPLIED_FACT: claim and entire frozen target frame.
- SOURCE_FACT: claim and entire frozen source instrument.
- DERIVED_INFERENCE: original cited mapping spans, frozen state/limit spans
  (to retain applicability and restrictions), source mechanism and target frame.
- CONDITIONAL_RELATION: the same frozen mapping context for the stated conditions
  and hypothetical outcome/consequence, source mechanism and target context.
  Do not invent or append observed results.
- OPERATION: the proposed operation in its original mapping context, source
  mechanism and target applicability/question; supplied context is not evidence
  that the proposed action has already succeeded.
- GOVERNANCE_RULE: the original mapping contains its trigger, action and explicitly
  cited basis; include source/target conditions for coherence. An authored threshold
  is not required to be empirically discovered. Missing trigger/action precision
  remains missing; do not manufacture it.
- LIMIT: original mapping context plus source mechanism and target structure
  relevant to the stated restriction.

For the five mapping-dependent contracts include original cited spans plus all
state/limit spans from that same mapping. Freeze this evidence menu with selection;
record any extra qualifier spans prospectively. Never retrieve unrelated packets.
This retains target applicability and restrictions without exposing the entire
operation/inference inventory. Cite appropriate spans and distinguish mapping
proposals from target observations. Existing prose is never enriched with new facts. Target/source fidelity packets omit
mapping unless a prospective protocol amendment is frozen before any measurement.
No such amendment is assumed or authorized by results.

## Execution and failure handling

Use gpt-6-astra, high reasoning, one ephemeral CLI session per claim and stage,
serial fixed manifest order, no duplicate measurements, tools, substitution or
harness retries. Verify local CLI/native hashes; exposed model identifiers are
recorded, and unavailable serving snapshots/internal retry details remain unknown.
Use an empty cwd, disabled rules/user configuration and disabled integrations.

Commit/hash each input checkpoint and recheck before every launch. Append-only
attempt evidence preserves command, schema, request, reservation, raw events,
response, validation and execution commit. Schema-invalid output is preserved and
pauses measurement without semantic repair. Citation errors in otherwise valid
records are scientific diagnostics, not reasons to retry or overwrite a verdict.

Any capacity/transport event pauses measurement and prevents further dispatch.
Report the event and missing observation without assigning a scientific result.
Do not define PROVIDER_NO_OBSERVATION/PROVIDER_OBSERVATION_FAILURE or inherit H.6's
permanent-termination rule. There is no retry/resume-on-provider-event command.
Engineering recovery follows docs/EXPERIMENT-RECOVERY-POLICY.md with positive
non-contamination evidence, committed restoration and applicable authority.
Lineage violations cannot be cleared by budget approval.

## Financial and allowance gates

Use scripts/experiment_budget.py with one stable experiment ID and shared ledger.
Reserve immediately before every provider launch. All failures/uncertain launches
retain their reservations. Forecast A=N and B<=N cumulatively, refining B only
once assignments freeze. No probes or billable preparation sessions.

Keep stages distinct for usage calibration. Fresh account snapshot and a remaining
work forecast are required at every stage/batch boundary, at most three judgments
per batch. Retain the cumulative financial plan. Reserve is 10 percentage points;
no dollar-to-quota conversion or reset-credit redemption. Unknown cost/allowance
requires actual user approval of the displayed concrete forecast. Approval records
must cite the user message and exact financial plan hash and reviewed usage report.
The runner never manufactures consent or automatically calls Gate.approve.

Save before/after snapshots, session counts and available token totals under
.runtime/. Exclude unverified account isolation, delayed telemetry, reset crossings
and saturation from clean calibration. Do not assume an unchanged percentage is
free work. Retain excluded observations and refresh the remaining forecast.

## Analysis, audit and limitations

After both stages freeze, report counts by assignment, role, contract and verdict;
operator/model atomicity and role concordance with explicit denominators; excluded
and uncertain cases; all anchor results; strict inference controls; and coverage
shortfalls. Count operator-role concordance only where an operator role and a
model assignment both exist, also reporting the all-selected agreement numerator.
A single observation per claim cannot establish repeatability or inter-rater
reliability. Curated corpus proportions do not estimate H.6-wide prevalence.

Only now join historical labels. For every anchor (and other selected claims where
available), retain old status and new role/verdict. Attribute contract mismatch
only with an explicit comparison of old failure reasoning to the appropriate new
contract; a verdict change alone is insufficient. Keep indeterminate cases
separate. CONDITIONAL is not an unconditional rescue. Missing historical judgments
remain missing, and coverage is not repaired post hoc.

Operator audit answers all twelve handoff questions: assignability, ambiguity,
atomization, target/source fidelity routing, governance coherence and false empirical
presentation, fallback scrutiny, hypothetical status, inference rejection,
historical contract mismatch, and new systematic ambiguity. Surface permissiveness,
wrong-contract failures and unavailable controls prominently; do not override
provider outputs. Recommend a next step without executing it.
