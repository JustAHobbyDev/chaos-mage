**Admission consequence:**

→ wrong local deletion/retention
→ false rejection or false retention of a generated transfer

**Minimum intervention:**

**Scientific success evidence:**
Complete end-to-end admission on fresh natural mappings; demonstrate that local violated claims can be isolated without automatically destroying viable source-derived machinery; demonstrate that surviving material contributions can be classified as KEEP, REDUCED, CORE, or UNCERTAIN for scientifically inspectable reasons.

**Stop condition:**

## Atomization, classification and obligations

One-primary-function propositions; no claim-count maximization. Preserve scope,
qualifiers, all five-field coverage and shared coarse-span references. Freeze claims
before classification, classifications before discovery, discovery before judgment.
An obviously compound frozen proposition is SPLIT_REQUIRED before evaluation. Stop
that lineage; no silent one-contract judgment or post-freeze semantic repair.

Origins: TARGET_SUPPLIED, SOURCE_SUPPLIED, BOTH_SUPPLIED, MAPPING_GENERATED.
Independent functions: FACT, INFERENCE, CONDITIONAL_RELATION, OPERATION,
GOVERNANCE_RULE, LIMIT. Provenance is a separate axis.
For supplied material, REFERENCED_PREMISE means unchanged scope/strength context;
ASSERTED_COMPONENT means the mapping prescribes, selects, applies, endorses,
transforms or materially extends it. Generated material has no supplied-premise
exemption. Freeze reasons and supporting sources, not just labels.

| Origin/mode/function | Required base contract(s) |
| --- | --- |
| supplied / REFERENCED_PREMISE | GROUNDING_REF |
| TARGET_SUPPLIED / ASSERTED_COMPONENT | TARGET_FIDELITY |
| SOURCE_SUPPLIED / ASSERTED_COMPONENT | SOURCE_FIDELITY |
| BOTH_SUPPLIED / ASSERTED_COMPONENT | TARGET_FIDELITY + SOURCE_FIDELITY |
| MAPPING_GENERATED / FACT | FACT_WARRANT |
| MAPPING_GENERATED / INFERENCE | DERIVED_WARRANT |
| MAPPING_GENERATED / CONDITIONAL_RELATION | CONDITIONAL_LICENSE |
| MAPPING_GENERATED / OPERATION | OPERATION_LICENSE |
| MAPPING_GENERATED / GOVERNANCE_RULE | GOVERNANCE_STRUCTURE |
| MAPPING_GENERATED / LIMIT | LIMIT_WARRANT |

Supplied asserted OPERATION adds OPERATION_LICENSE to fidelity (including both
fidelities for BOTH_SUPPLIED). A referenced unchanged operation gets GROUNDING_REF,
without unnecessary functional obligation.

GOVERNANCE_STRUCTURE contract:

> Is this proposition genuinely a prospective decision rule with a sufficiently determinate trigger and sufficiently determinate prescribed response, and is the decision structure internally coherent at the stated scope? Judge only the rule's decision structure. Do not evaluate whether embedded operations are informative, mechanism-licensed, effective, causally appropriate, or target-relevant, and do not evaluate the truth or warrant of embedded factual, inferential, conditional, or limiting premises; those belong to separate obligations.

Never use GOVERNANCE_COHERENCE. Discover direct secondary obligations only when
DISTINCT, MATERIAL and UNEVALUATED. Establish content_origin, epistemic_function,
assertion_mode first; then resolve as GROUNDING_REF, CLAIM_REF or INLINE_OBLIGATION.
No EMBEDDED_FACT shortcut. One level only; a materially independent further level
requires SPLIT_REQUIRED. CLAIM_REF reuses the frozen required judgment, not a new
sample. Discovery is one session per mapping; evaluation one session per unique
required obligation, with actual fan-out determined only after discovery.

Every required contract receives SATISFIED, CONDITIONAL, VIOLATED or UNCERTAIN.
Evaluate all obligations, even after a failure. Aggregate by
VIOLATED > UNCERTAIN > CONDITIONAL > SATISFIED. No universal supported verdict.

## Supplied-operation watch and fidelity failures

For every TARGET_SUPPLIED or SOURCE_SUPPLIED asserted OPERATION (also monitor
BOTH_SUPPLIED), record claim_id, content_origin, fidelity_contract,
fidelity_verdict, operation_license_present, operation_license_verdict,
routing_clean, admission_effect. Include direct secondary operations and distinguish
their obligation identity. This is observational, not another calibration.

Unestablished routing or operation scrutiny yields instrumentation FAILURE and
scientific admission UNMEASURED; do not admit, call CORE_INVALID, or relabel it
UNCERTAIN_LOAD_BEARING. Preserve the case. A supplied-origin fidelity failure is a
classification/measurement problem, not evidence that the proposition is false.
Never silently reroute. Review only frozen evidence; if admission materially depends
on an unresolved fidelity problem, quarantine. No replacement model observation.

## Ablation and remainder

Freeze all measurable claim judgments before ablation. Delete generated claims
with overall VIOLATED. Remove a supplied operation's mapping-prescribed use only
when required fidelity is SATISFIED and OPERATION_LICENSE VIOLATED; retain source
fact in source record. A secondary generated violation removes its dependent
mapping assertion/use, not the underlying supplied fact. Record exact claim and
obligation lineage and the decision. Preserve CONDITIONAL/UNCERTAIN with flags.
No subset search, replacement inference or semantic repair. Ablation is deletion
of frozen semantic claim objects, not model-authored byte surgery. Shared spans
confer no ownership; deleted premises cannot leak back through original prose.

Inventory surviving material across all five fields: positive target inferences,
TARGET_CONSTRAINT, INQUIRY_CONSTRAINT, INSUFFICIENCY_ONLY, limits, operations,
stopping conditions and source-derived distinctions. Discovery is polarity neutral.
H.4 complete inquiry = unresolved contrast + specific next operation + discriminating
outcome relation + sufficient provenance. H.3 productive negative remainder changes
target states, bounds, relative priorities, stopping decision or specific discriminating
next inquiry. Mere lack of evidence for X cannot rescue an artifact.

Assess viability in order: warranted AND source-derived AND target-relevant AND
productive AND material. Report YES/NO/UNCERTAIN with inspectable reasons and
provenance. Neither surviving text volume, graph centrality nor focal-sentence
ownership substitutes for these criteria. One inventory session per mapping,
then freeze all inventories; one isolated artifact session per measurable mapping.

## UNMEASURED recovery boundary

Repository docs/EXPERIMENT-RECOVERY-POLICY.md governs. Pause scheduling on every
invariant violation and preserve in-flight responses. Record the exact affected
mapping/stage, input/response hashes, session and execution identity, failure,
reservations and state. Provider observations never change.

1. Local observed routing, fidelity, unknown-response-source or uninterpretable
   response failure: quarantine that mapping as FAILURE / UNMEASURED. It consumes
   its slot; no retry or replacement and no scientific rejection.
2. Before any unrelated launch, establish positive non-contamination evidence:
   exact frozen hash equality; explicit input allowlists and dependency closure;
   independent session/context identities; no shared scientific input/config/schema
   change or leaked failed output. Commit incident and recovery evidence, reverify
   historical preservation and clean continuation, name the resume checkpoint.
   A mere assertion of independence or different mapping ID is insufficient.
3. If independence is established, continue only unaffected frozen mapping lineages
   under existing authority and fresh budget/usage checks. Preserve the quarantined
   artifact and all earlier pauses. Recovery of engineering bookkeeping may retain
   the same original observation only with positive proof it was unaffected.
4. Shared or unresolved impact: pause as PAUSED_AMBIGUOUS while investigating;
   unresolved ambiguity is measurement_lineage_violation. Invalidate affected stage
   and terminate as TERMINATED_LINEAGE. Explicit new recovery/restart authority is
   required. No automatic clearing of a terminal state.
5. Capacity/transport: pause, preserve, no retry, no substitution, report. H7 creates
   no provider-no-observation continuation policy, even if other mappings appear
   independent. Uncertain launch status blocks continuation. Budget reservations
   survive failed/uncertain launches.

State evidence is append-only: RUNNING, PAUSED_RECOVERABLE, PAUSED_AMBIGUOUS,
TERMINATED_LINEAGE, TERMINATED_MEASUREMENT, COMPLETE. A complete run record is
distinct from six complete measurements; report both. Quarantine cannot silently
turn a failed stage into success.

admission, or genuinely indeterminate packet support. Formatting/roles alone do not

operations, supplied facts receiving generated burdens, supplied operations escaping
